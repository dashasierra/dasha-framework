# Windows

The window in front is the active one. It has a double frame, and what you type goes to it.

## Moving between windows

**F6** brings the next window to the front, and **Shift+F6** the previous one. Clicking a window also brings it forward.

## Moving and resizing

Drag a window's title bar to move it, and the grip at its bottom-right corner to resize it, if it has one.

From the keyboard, **Alt+F7** moves the active window and **Alt+F8** resizes it:

1. Use the arrow keys to move it or change its size.
2. Press **Tab** to switch between moving and resizing.
3. Press **Enter** to keep the result, or **Esc** to put the window back.

## Maximising, minimising and closing

A window shows the buttons it allows on its title bar while it is active:

| Button | What it does | Key |
|---|---|:---:|
| `[↑]` | Fills the screen with the window | F5 |
| `[↕]` | Restores it to its earlier size | F5 |
| `[↓]` | Minimises it | none by default |
| `[■]` | Closes it | Alt+F4 or Alt+F3 |

Double-clicking the title bar also maximises or restores the window. A minimised window leaves the screen, and is listed in its application's **Window** menu (or, for one of the desktop's own windows, in the [system menu](menus.md#the-system-menu)), from which it can be restored.

## Scrolling

When a window's contents do not fit, scroll bars appear on its frame. The arrow keys, **PgUp**, **PgDn** and the mouse wheel scroll it too.

These keys can all be changed: see [the keyboard](keyboard.md).
