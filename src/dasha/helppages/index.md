# Dasha Desktop Help

Dasha Desktop is a desktop of overlapping windows in a terminal. Each application runs on its own, and they share the desktop.

Press **Tab** to move to a link below, and **Enter** to follow it.

## Topics

- [Windows](windows.md): moving, resizing and switching between them
- [Menus](menus.md): the menu bar and the system menu
- [The keyboard](keyboard.md): the keys that work everywhere
- [Applications](applications.md): starting them and switching between them
- [Running a program](running.md): Run... in the system menu
- [Settings](settings.md): colours, keys and everything else you can change

## Getting around help

The row at the top of this window goes **Back** and **Forward** through the pages you have read, to the **Index**, and to **Search**, which looks through every topic. The keys for each are listed on the status bar.

Applications can have help of their own. Press **F1** in one, and if it has any, its help opens at the topic for whatever you are working on.
