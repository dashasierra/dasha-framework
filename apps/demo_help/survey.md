# The survey

The **Ask...** button opens a dialog, which is why this page opened in front of it. Close this help with **Esc** to go back to the dialog, with your answers as you left them.

## Do you like Dasha Framework

Choose one answer. The radio buttons name this heading as their topic, with `rating.help = "survey#do-you-like-dasha-framework"`.
